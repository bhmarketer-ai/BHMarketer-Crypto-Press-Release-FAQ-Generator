# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release: 50 questions and answers about buying a crypto press release from BHMarketer.ai (`crypto_pr_faq/faq.json`) in six categories, each with keywords and search phrases.
- `ask`, `top`, `show`, `list`, `categories`, `export`, `html`, `jsonld` and `seo` commands in Node.js and Python.
- Questions ranked by how often they are asked, with a "Most Asked Questions" table.
- Page FAQ section (`output/faq-section.html`): one H2, an H3 per question, visible answers and matching FAQPage JSON-LD.
- Page SEO settings for the Buy Crypto Press Release page in `crypto_pr_faq/config.json` and `output/seo.json`.
- `llms.txt` and `AGENTS.md` for AI systems and AI coding tools.
